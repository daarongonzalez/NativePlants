StickyNote is a small tilted note for an aside, a tip or a headline fact.

**You provide:** a short mono kicker (e.g. `PSST`, `GOOD TO KNOW`) or a big figure, one or two lines of text and, for a figure, its source.

**Anatomy**
- Fill `peach`, `sky` or `meadow`. `border-2` in `line`, `--radius-note` (16) to `--radius-card` (24), `--shadow-4` to `--shadow-6`.
- Tilted with a `--tilt-*` token, between −3° and +2°.
- Kicker in mono at `--type-mono-sm`, upper case. Figures in display type at `--type-figure`, in `green-deep`. Sources in mono at `--type-mono-sm`.

**Do**
- Tilt neighbouring notes in opposite directions.
- Keep the text short enough to read at an angle.
- Always give a figure its source.

**Don't**
- Put form fields, buttons or long text on a note.
- Use more than three notes in one view.

Live example: the PSST note and the water facts on the landing page.
