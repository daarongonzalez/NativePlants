# NativePlants

A planning tool for home gardeners, community gardeners, and anyone growing
flowers or produce in their own yard.

The idea: take what is actually knowable about a specific patch of ground — its
soil, its climate, its sun — and turn that into a real planting plan, then
connect that plan to a local nursery that has the plants in stock.

## Status

Early. Research and planning only, no application code yet.
